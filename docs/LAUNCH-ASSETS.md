## LinkedIn draft

> Vendor terms and privacy policies often change quietly, while procurement and compliance teams are busy doing everything else. I built Policy Change Monitor on Apify to keep dated baselines, strip page noise, show exact before-and-after clauses, and flag likely changes to pricing, data use, retention, liability, termination, arbitration, API limits, and SLAs. It costs $0.004 per public page check and needs no external AI key. I am looking for two procurement, privacy, or vendor-risk teams willing to test a 25-vendor monitoring pack and tell me where the workflow still creates friction.

## Apify community draft

The differentiator is clause-level evidence and deterministic materiality classification rather than a generic page diff. I would value feedback on the input flow and scheduled-task setup, especially from anyone building compliance or vendor-risk automations.

## Direct pilot invitation

> I am testing a vendor-policy monitoring workflow for procurement and compliance teams. If you send a list of up to 25 public terms, privacy, DPA, SLA, or pricing URLs, the tool will create baselines and return only meaningful clause changes with evidence. There is no login or private data involved. Would a 15-minute setup review be useful?

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the 60-second product demo and the Alexa+ prototype story.
2. Post to LinkedIn and tag Apify only after final review.
3. Ask for two 25-vendor pilots, with no free custom development promise.
4. Turn the first real workflow reaction into a Store review or short case study, with explicit permission.
5. Submit the same evidence-backed demo to Product Hunt only after activation and reliability data exist.
